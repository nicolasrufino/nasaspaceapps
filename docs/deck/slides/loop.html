MISSION VESTA · VESTA SCOUT

The workflow in the current build

1

Map

HiRISE imagery on relief; a Hazards view with its legend.

2

Plan

Five selected science stops and a route back to base.

3

Scout

At most three flights, each up to 1 km along the current route.

4

Re-plan

After each flight, avoid what was found and choose the next unseen stretch.

5

Walk

Watch the return margin; inspect a stop and record a field note.

app/src/main.js, scout.js, plan.js, walk.js; ml/exports/pitch_metrics.json.

1:15–1:35. The map uses real orbital products. The helicopter is simulated and reveals the stored hazard layer. The route changes after every landing; the walk itself reads the precomputed return field. Sources: Ingenuity as an aerial scout for rover scientists and drivers https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/after-three-years-on-mars-nasas-ingenuity-helicopter-mission-ends/ ; flight 12 scouted for the rover at 10 m with stereo pairs https://science.nasa.gov/blog/better-by-the-dozen-ingenuity-takes-on-flight-12/ ; SkyFall flights of 1 to 2 km https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/skyfall-mission-information-package-final.pdf
