MARTIAN MAP · NASA SPACE APPS 2026

Arizona, 2022

NASA astronauts rehearse a moonwalk with the maps Artemis will actually have. On the very first walk, a slope those maps couldn’t show blocks the route, and the walk ends early.

That was the Moon, with help seconds away. On Mars, help is 20 minutes away.

0:00 to 0:30. NASA's Joint EVA Test Team, JETT3, near Flagstaff, October 2022. They used 1 m images and a 1.5 m elevation model on purpose, to match what Artemis expects. EVA 1 hit slopes over 16 degrees and drop-offs and ended short of its goals. Source: NASA JETT3 test report (NTRS 20230010686).
