WHAT NASA GAVE US, AND HOW WE USE IT

One 2 × 2 km slice of Mount Sharp, built from NASA’s resources

ResourceWhat it isHow we use it
Mars TrekJPL’s web map of Mars with stackable layersPick our slice; download context layers
USGS geologic mapsWhere each rock unit isScience targets; the same USGS site gives us 1 m terrain and 25 cm images of Gale
Curiosity’s route mapNASA’s planned rover path up Mount SharpModel for our route view; its regions are our targets; a benchmark
Perseverance raw imagesOver a million rover photos from JezeroTest our ground classifier on a second rover’s camera
NASA WorldviewEarth satellite imageryNot used: it has no Mars data

Also added: THEMIS (sand), CRISM (minerals), Curiosity raw images, RAD and REMS (radiation, weather), DONKI (storms), AI4Mars (classifier labels). Full guide: docs/DATA.md.

Use if judges ask how we used the challenge resources, or keep in the flow after the data slide. Say plainly that Worldview is Earth data and we did not force it in; honesty about data scores on Validity.
