HOW WE KNOW IT CAN WORK

Every piece is already proven at NASA. We put them together for the crew.

Rocks

Shadow-based rock detection on HiRISE chose or checked the Phoenix, Gale, Jezero and InSight landing sites.

Scout

Ingenuity flew 72 times and scouted routes for Perseverance; one flight saved about two months of driving.

Path

Perseverance’s AutoNav picks its own safe path between stops.

Home

Walkback limits and the 1-hour return rule keep crews safe today.

Sources: JPL Phoenix landing site characterization; Golombek et al. 2021; Planetary Society, Ingenuity; NASA, How Perseverance drives; NASA Tenets of Lunar EVA (2023).
