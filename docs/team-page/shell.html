Martian Map Team Plan

Martian Map turns NASA's Mars data into a science walk you can plan, preview and come home from, and makes the map better with every walk.

The problem

There is no map at the scale of one walk. NASA's orbital data shows the region but misses the rock field, the soft sand and the slope that decide whether a suited astronaut gets through, and on Mars help is 4 to 22 minutes away.

Our solution: a map that builds itself, walk by walk

  1. 1 OrbitNASA's maps of the whole site: where the science is, where not to go, where to look closer
  2. 2 ScoutA drone flies the route first and finds the rocks and sand orbit can't see
  3. 3 Plan and packSafe, efficient route, margin home, packing list, decided before leaving
  4. 4 Walk and addThe suit display keeps the way home known; every step adds detail to the map
  5. 5 Next walkThe next crew starts from a better map and goes further

Coarse to fine: each stage sees more detail than the one before, covers less ground and costs more, so each one tells the next where to look.
