What the app could look like

A suggested design, not a decision. The order follows a Marswalk: plan, scout and pack, walk, investigate, and stay safe. The maps below are drawn from the real output of make_world.py (the synthetic world): terrain, terrain type and the energy needed to walk home from every point. The phone screen stands in for a suit display, so it is dark, high-contrast and readable at arm's length while walking. The map data is real; the numbers on the screens (margins, minutes, kilojoules) are illustrative.
